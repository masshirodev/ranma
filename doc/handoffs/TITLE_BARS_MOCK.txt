################ Findings ################

## Title bars: what the proposal cannot express
F1  Groups draw two rows
    DESIGN.md decides "Groups keep their tab bar", and a grouped pane's top edge is drawn like any other. With a filled title row every group then shows its tab row over its title row (Window, "as proposed"), and the brief's rule is one row.
    Ask: tabs.position = "title" (NEW): the tabs drawn in the active pane's title row, where `title` would be, and the tab row's cell given back to the pane. Or a built-in `tabs` piece: a theme without it keeps today's tab row.
F2  No text colour on a state's ground
    c437 gives each state one colour. Without side edges the row's ground is the only place a state can show, and then its text has no role. An urgent badge in bar_urgent on an urgent ground disappears.
    Ask: title_fg_urgent and title_fg_synced (NEW, used in Window and Pills), or state pairs (R3). On a state ground, badges take the row's text colour.
F3  One precedence, and a colour clash
    Edges and title rows need one order: WM mode, urgent, synced, zoomed, scrolled, then focused / floating / inactive. In the built-in theme mode_bg and bar_urgent are the same colour, so an urgent pane that fell back to bar_urgent would look like the WM-mode pane.
    Ask: Record the order, and give border_urgent a default that is never mode_bg (the mocks use a peach).
F4  Thin side edges
    ▏ on the left and ▕ on the right are two characters. border.chars has one vertical, and top_chars / bottom_chars only cover the top and bottom.
    Ask: Left and right characters of their own (R2).
F5  A float with no edges blends in
    An unfocused float takes inactive_bg, the same ground as the tiles under it, and with no edge its left side runs into them. The shadow only covers the right and bottom.
    Ask: panes.floating_bg (NEW).
F6  A pill round one piece
    title_cap_* take the row's corners, which rounds the whole row (Window). A pill round the title alone, round each badge, or round the buttons together (Pills) cannot be made, because setting title_bg_* fills the whole row.
    Ask: border.title_ground = "row" | "title" (NEW). Then piece caps with their own ground (border.piece_cap_*, colors.piece_bg / piece_fg, NEW), and a list inside a ranma.titlebar side drawn as one pill, as c445 does for the bar. Better still: apply c444's caps per role to title pieces too, so a piece with style = "x" takes [roles.x]'s ground and caps. Then only title_ground and the grouping are new.
F7  Right side first starves the title
    Budgeting as the bar does keeps every button and cuts the left side at its end. Badges, urgent ones included, go before the title is cut, and at 18 columns the pane shows three buttons with no title and no ⇉ (the ladders). That breaks the proposal's own "the marks a config did not list still show".
    Ask: keep / a priority on a piece (NEW), and a ladder that mixes sides: badge words, the title cut to four letters, calm badges, float, zoom, the title's name (⇉ and the icon stay), urgent badges, with close and the index last. Drawn as "this handoff's ladder".
F8  A handle lacks what pieces need
    render(pane) gets a pane handle, which has no index, zoomed, synced or urgent. So Quiet's index, a zoom button that reads "restore" while zoomed, and a title that follows the bell cannot be written.
    Ask: Those four fields on handles.
F9  A colour cannot name a role
    Quiet's title_bg_* repeat the pane grounds, and every hover role repeats a button colour, all as hex. A matugen template then has to learn every new key.
    Ask: Let a colour be another role's name (title_bg_active = "active_bg", NEW), resolved after the merge.
F10  WM mode on a row of blanks
    With top_chars blank and title_bg_* unset, the row is a border line of spaces, so mode_bg lands on the title's letters only (Quiet, "as proposed").
    Ask: A rule, not a key: a focused row with no line to colour is filled with mode_bg / mode_fg.
F11  Switching themes keeps init.lua's layout
    choose_theme from Window to Quiet keeps three buttons on a theme meant to have none, and from Classic to Grouped it keeps the groups of one.
    Ask: Let a theme name its pieces ([titlebar] right = [...], with ranma.title_piece still defining them), or have the docs say that a look is a theme plus init.lua lines.
F12  No scrollbar without a right edge
    Decided in the proposal, and it holds.
    Ask: With panes.padding_right of 1 or more, the thumb could sit in the padding column.

## Title bars: what to rename
R1  title_cap_left / right → title_corner_left / right
    They take the corners and round the row, not the title. I read them as caps round the title on the first pass, which is the reading the name invites.
R2  top_chars, bottom_chars, chars → one table
    [border.chars] top = "╭─╮", bottom = "╰─╯", left = "│", right = "│". This fixes F4, and custom becomes "any side set".
R3  c437's border_urgent… → state_urgent + state_urgent_fg …
    In an edgeless theme they colour a row's ground and its text, not a border. This fixes F2.
R4  hover_style: keep
    It is the same word for title pieces and bar modules (c446), and reads well in both.

## Title bars: what to drop
D1  bottom_chars
    No look here needed it. It folds into R2.
D2  border.edges = []
    It is the same as style = "none". Refuse it, or make it the only spelling.
D3  title_align with ranma.titlebar
    The slots decide where things go, so setting both should be an error.
D4  top_chars' corners on a filled row
    The fill covers them, and title_cap_* take them anyway. Document them as "corners only on an unfilled row", or drop them.

## Bars: what c444-c446 cannot express
B1  Built-in modules take no role
    style comes from a Lua module's return value. cpu, mem, session and workspaces have none, so the classic bar makes each one a group of one just to give it a ground.
    Ask: style on every built-in: ranma.module("cpu", { style = "cpu" }).
B2  The mode's ground
    In arrows mode the mode module is one chip. The arrow after it must be drawn in mode_bg, but a module's ground comes from its role.
    Ask: A rule: a module whose text is a single chip takes the chip's ground (as drawn).
B3  Cuts must keep caps, and the centre goes first
    c445's "cut with … inside its caps" is right and needed: today's truncate cuts the flat piece list, so the closing cap or arrow goes (the "today" rows). In arrows mode the cut segment should keep its arrow. Because the centre gets only what the sides leave, workspaces moved there are cut first: Dots at 80 columns keeps only the current dot.
    Ask: A floor for the centre of its own (fit_floor already exists for one case), or keep on a module.
B4  A hover format loses the state
    A single workspace_hover_format replaces the empty / occupied / urgent glyph, so the dot under the pointer stops saying what it is.
    Ask: Drop it. Hover becomes ws_hover_fg / ws_hover_bg over the state's own format (as drawn).
B5  One separator colour, two grounds
    colors.separator colours bar.separator on bar_bg and group_separator on a pill's ground, and a pill on a role's ground (sys) makes a third. One colour cannot read on all of them.
    Ask: colors.group_separator (NEW), or a separator in the group's role, [roles.x] separator (NEW).
B6  Chip caps per side
